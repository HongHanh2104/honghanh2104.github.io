# About Me

I'm a second-year Ph.D student at [University College Dublin (UCD)](https://www.ucd.ie/), under superivsion of [Assoc. Prof. Nhien-An Le-Khac](https://people.ucd.ie/an.lekhac) who is a great supervisor and scientist. I got full scholarship for 4-year Ph.D program from [SFI Centre for Research Training in Machine Learning](https://www.ml-labs.ie/). I'm also a member of [UCD Artificial Intelligence Solution for Cyber Security, Digital Forensics and Smart Systems (ASEADOS) Lab](https://aseados.ucd.ie/), which is a research group focusing on AI security, cybersecurity, and digital forensics.

My main research focus on the area of deepfake detection and adversarial ML. I'm also interested in the application of AI in cybersecurity and digital forensics. Adaptive Learning is also a research orientation that I'm interested in. My Ph.D research topic is "Generalized Deepfake Detection" which aims to develop a generalized deepfake detection model that can adapt to various deepfake generation methods or post-processing operations.

# Past Study
I received my B.Sc. degree in Computer Science from the Honors Program of the [Department of Information Technology, University of Science, Vietnam National University, Ho Chi Minh City](https://www.fit.hcmus.edu.vn/) in 2020. I was a member of Decentralized Applied Cryptography Lab (DAC-lab@FIT), advised by [Assoc. Prof. Dinh Thuc Nguyen](https://www.fit.hcmus.edu.vn/~ndthuc/). During the time in this lab, I worked on projects related to voice-based biometric template protection.
